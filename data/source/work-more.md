Three more shipped products
SPEED DATING · 2025
Randevmeste
A platform for offline speed-dating events where guests meet up to 30 matches a night in short
rounds. The client had outgrown a Wix-only setup with no guest tracking, real-time
communication, filtering or notifications.
What CloseFuture built. A custom Bubble app integrated with Wix over API — tickets bought
on Wix flow straight into the database. Guests register by ticket number, build profiles, and move
through event phases with real-time liking and matching, instant match alerts, secure post-event
messaging, safety tools, and host controls.
"The app has exceeded our expectations both in terms of design and functionality." — Jozef
Greftak, client
WOMEN'S FITNESS · 2025
Vigo
A fitness app built specifically for women — personalized workouts, meal tracking and AI nutrition
analysis — because generic fitness apps miss the mark on personalization and motivation.
What CloseFuture built. Goal-based onboarding and plans; real-time activity/calorie tracking
that adjusts dynamically; custom diet plans with portion guidance; AI meal analysis through a
WhatsApp bot (log a meal with a photo); and subscriptions via RevenueCat. Launched 2025 in
Israel, with strong retention.
CREATOR COMMUNITY · 2025
Galaxy Move
A mobile-first community platform connecting Samsung creators and fans through gamified
engagement, built because existing tools lacked interactivity and scale.
What CloseFuture built. A gamified task-and-points system, community posting with direct
admin contact, a real-time leaderboard, role-based admin controls (super admins vs. limited
community managers), and reward redemption for gifts and coupons. Delivered as mobile and web
apps, boosting engagement and brand loyalty.
