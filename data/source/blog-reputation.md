Building scalable apps with FlutterFlow
21 May 2026
A guide to architecting FlutterFlow apps that grow from MVP to thousands of users. The core argument:
scalability is a shared job between a strong backend (Supabase, Firebase or custom APIs doing the heavy
lifting) and a disciplined frontend. It walks through backend-first architecture, clean relational data
models, pagination and lazy loading, consistent naming, careful state management, reusable
components, and row-level security — the difference between a demo and something production-ready.
Neon vs Supabase: a 2026 choosing & migration guide
29 April 2026
A practical comparison of two Postgres platforms: Neon, a serverless database with autoscaling and
branching, versus Supabase, a full backend bundling auth, storage, realtime and auto-generated APIs.
The piece weighs authentication, realtime, branching, scale-to-zero economics and pricing, then lands on
a clear rule of thumb — Neon for composable, bursty, ephemeral setups; Supabase for fast MVPs and
standard SaaS — and notes that migrating between them is easy in theory but 1–3 weeks of real work.
