UGC MARKETPLACE · ISRAEL · 2026
Dipy — a marketplace for user-generated content
Dipy is an Israeli marketplace that connects brand owners with content creators. Brands post orders for
videos, photos or graphics; creators browse, apply, produce the work and get paid. Simple on the surface
— but the market it entered was anything but.
THE PROBLEM
Existing UGC solutions were fragmented. A brand wanting content had to stitch together separate tools
for discovering creators, running quality assurance, managing revisions, handling payments and staying
compliant on the legal side. Creators had it no easier — there was no single place to find good
opportunities and keep an eye on what they were actually earning.
WHAT CLOSEFUTURE BUILT
A production-grade platform on Bubble, split into four purpose-built surfaces so each user gets exactly
the interface they need:
Brand-owner desktop — a command hub for creating orders, searching the creator database,
sending direct invitations and tracking everything in flight.
Content-creator mobile — a native-feeling interface tuned to a 420px width, for browsing orders,
applying, uploading work, handling revisions and tracking earnings.
Brand-owner mobile — a touch-optimized version for managing orders and talking to creators on
the go.
Admin panel — creator verification, order oversight and payment processing for the operations
team.
THE DETAILS THAT MATTERED
AI semantic search matching creators to orders by language, interests and personal characteristics —
cutting the time brands spend choosing.
Full bilingual support in Hebrew and English.
Automated payments handling VAT, discount codes and partial credits.
Notifications across four channels: push, in-app messaging, WhatsApp and email.
A creator level-progression system that unlocks higher-value opportunities over time.
Direct creator invitations running alongside organic applications.
Outcome — Dipy launched as a production marketplace handling hundreds of concurrent orders,
with approvals and payments running largely on their own.

EMPLOYEE WELLBEING · AI · 2026
Liya AI — a wellbeing platform that listens
Liya AI is an intelligent employee-wellbeing platform for stress management. It blends conversational AI,
real-time surveys and analytics with access to real human therapists — automation where it helps, people
where it matters.
THE PROBLEM
Organizations couldn't see stress and burnout building until it was too late. Traditional surveys arrived
too slowly to act on. Employees held back for fear of being identified. And HR teams had no realistic way
to read and act on large volumes of open feedback.
WHAT CLOSEFUTURE BUILT
Liya Coach — a conversational AI assistant supporting both text and voice, so employees can raise
concerns in whatever way feels safe.
Survey management — tools to create and send surveys that capture mood and engagement in real
time.
Voice-enabled interaction — natural-language input for intuitive, low-friction use.
Automated summarization — AI that turns raw survey data into clear, actionable insight.
Analytics dashboard — interactive visual reporting with advanced filters for spotting trends across
teams and segments.
The platform ships as both web and mobile applications, and was completed in 2026.
Outcome — better visibility into wellbeing and faster, data-driven decisions. Voice and chat
pushed participation up, while therapist integration kept the support genuinely personal — feeding
into higher satisfaction and a healthier workplace.

SMART OFFICE · IOT · ISRAEL · 2026
Webiz — the oﬃce, on demand
Webiz lets professionals and companies book premium offices and meeting rooms by the hour, with no
long-term contract. What makes it interesting is that the software reaches past the screen and into the
building itself.
THE PROBLEM
Traditional office rentals are rigid, expensive and a poor fit for how people actually work now. Companies
pay for space that sits empty, and juggle access, scheduling and billing across systems that don't talk to
each other.
WHAT CLOSEFUTURE BUILT
A complete ecosystem — a mobile app, an admin dashboard and an on-site tablet application — sitting on
a backend that unifies discovery, booking, payments and device control into one flow:
QR-code instant booking — tablets on site show codes for immediate access and real-time
availability checks.
Flexible scheduling — hourly, future-dated, extended and permanent bookings.
Company wallet — a shared team credit pool with member management.
Dynamic pricing — bonus credit packages, per-order discounts and auto-renewal.
Secure payments — integrated through Rivhit.
IoT control — automated doors, electricity and air-conditioning, all from the app.
Outcome — launched July 2026 in Israel, turning rigid office rentals into flexible, self-service
access and automating what used to be manual building management.
