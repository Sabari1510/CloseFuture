# Services and process
_Source: closefuture-profile.pdf › Services and process_

What CloseFuture does
End-to-end product development, from the ﬁrst whiteboard sketch to long-term care.
WEB & MOBILE APPS
Full builds — web apps, mobile apps, dashboards,
tablet and kiosk interfaces — on low-code
foundations with custom backends where needed.
PRODUCT DESIGN & UX
Design is treated as a specialty. Requirements
become responsive, polished interfaces, refined
with the client until the flow feels right.
AI-INTEGRATED SOLUTIONS
Chat and voice assistants, semantic search and
matching, automated summarization, and
photo/image analysis woven into real workflows.
CUSTOM PLATFORMS
Bespoke systems that pull a scattered, manual
process into one product — booking, payments,
notifications and access in a single place.
AUTOMATION & INTEGRATIONS
Workflow automation with n8n, plus integrations
for Wix, payment gateways (Rivhit, RevenueCat),
WhatsApp, notifications and IoT devices.
MAINTENANCE & SUPPORT
Long-term care — performance, upgrades and
adapting the product as the audience grows —
delivered across time zones.
